How certification works

  1. Join

    Receive your roll number and a 6-digit joining code, then create your login.

  2. Learn

    Attend classes, read the handbook and tick off topics in your student portal.

  3. Be assessed

    Module tests, coach-marked practicals and a viva or live practical check.

  4. Certify

    Pass and receive a YKFA Combat Federation certificate with a unique number to verify.

Your student portal

Everything in one private login: your assigned course content, attendance and leave requests, progress tracker, mock tests and results.

Tests are timed and randomised, and the portal records activity during exams to keep results fair.

Taught by working coaches

Led by the YKFA head coach, with specialist coaches for boxing, wrestling, karate, MMA, Muay Thai and fitness. You learn from people who coach every week.

Questions

Do I need any experience?

No. The beginner programme starts from the basics. Athletes can join the weekend programme.

Is the online certificate the same as the offline one?

The online programme covers the same theory. Online students complete a live practical check before the certificate is issued.

Will this make me a licensed coach?

This is a YKFA Combat Federation certificate. Rules for opening academies and coaching licences differ by place, and the course teaches you what to check.

Can I use the content to teach others?

Course material is for enrolled students only and may not be shared or copied. Every page in the portal carries the student's name.

What do I need to bring to classes?

Comfortable training clothes, water and a towel. Bring gloves and a mouthguard for striking modules, or ask us for the list.
